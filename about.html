What is Mental Math Lab?

Mental Math Lab is a website made to help people get faster and more confident at mental math. It teaches different tricks that can make calculations easier to do in your head.

Why I made it

I made Mental Math Lab because I wasn't very good at mental math. I would sometimes use a calculator for really simple things that I probably could have figured out in my head. I wanted to get better at it, so I decided to make a website where I could learn different mental math tricks and have them all in one place.

Inspired by

Mental Math Lab was inspired by The Secrets of Mental Math by Arthur Benjamin. I learned a lot of the tricks and ideas from the book, which gave me the inspiration for this project.

How it works

Pick a trick from the Tricks Library and learn how it works through a few examples. Then, you can practice it with randomly generated problems. The tricks are also organized by category and difficulty to make it easier to find what you want to learn.
